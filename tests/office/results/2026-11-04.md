# Office files, measured 4 Nov 2026

their sources that day), from ministries and municipalities, opened and saved by the ZENOS office engine (`zenos`:
system/lib/zoffice.py, LibreOffice 25.2.3 of Debian 13 in the ZENOS sandbox), and compared with the originals
(tests/office/compat.py, run by .github/workflows/office-compat.yml). The pages of both files are drawn by
LibreOffice; Microsoft Office is not used, so "look alike" means alike after saving, not alike in Microsoft Office.
