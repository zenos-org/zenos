Features / Studio

Testing

A small studio for music and pictures, that only touches everyday files.

For the Personal and Family editions: a library of your music and pictures, a trimmer with fades, a picture placed on a song, and one MP4 out. If you connect your own AI, it can turn the picture into a short moving clip.

L1 only

sealed files are never listed, opened or sent

Your AI

ZENOS ships no video model

1 MP4

out, and the original is never overwritten

How it works

Pick, trim, export

1

Pick

The library shows music and pictures from your everyday places only: Basic, Music, Pictures and Videos.

  • Playlists and albums are kept on the PC
2

Trim

Set the start and end on the waveform, add a fade, and choose a picture for the song.

3

Export

One MP4 in Full HD. With your own AI connected, the picture can move under the music.

  • Every AI request is recorded
  • An administrator can turn the AI part off

Proven, and what is not

What is checked, and what is trusted

Tested

Checked before anything leaves

  • Only L1 pictures may go to an AI; sealed and higher files are refused
  • Only HTTPS addresses are used, except your own PC for testing
  • A policy setting turns the AI part off
Trusted, not proved

Tested and listed openly

  • ffmpeg, which does the audio and video work
  • The AI service you connect
