Developer Preview · not yet for real classified data

A desktop operating system for classified work — that ordinary staff can use.

Every file carries a security level. Opening above your level asks your officers to approve. Anything that looks like a leak stops the machine until several officers release it from their own devices. Open source, no single vendor.

DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Security that does not need training

Familiar first

A Windows-like layout and ordinary controls. Staff work the way they already do; the protection sits underneath.

Nobody acts alone

Keys above your level are issued only after approval. Releasing a stopped PC needs several officers, each signing on their own device.

History cannot be rewritten

Every action goes into a hash-chained ledger kept on audit nodes run by different parties. A forked history is caught.

No single vendor

Debian, KDE and WebKitGTK. Open source, self-hostable, inspectable by anyone.
